Pydantic AI has one prompt caching setting that works across providers. 💸

Agent runs resend the whole conversation on every step. Several providers, Anthropic included, cache nothing unless the request asks them to, so you pay full price for your instructions, tools and history every time. Add Caching() to your agent's capabilities, and Pydantic AI places the cache breakpoints each provider needs.

I tried it with Claude Sonnet 5.5 and 14k tokens of docs as instructions. Without caching, the request cost $0.030. With Caching(), the follow-up read the 14k tokens from the cache and cost $0.0034.

- It applies to Anthropic, Bedrock, OpenRouter, and OpenAI GPT-5.6 and later.
- Caching(messages=False) caches only the stable prefix, for many short one-off conversations.
- Caching('1h') asks for a longer retention, snapped to what the provider supports.
- Cache writes cost more than uncached input, which is why caching is opt-in.
