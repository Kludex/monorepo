I killed the coding agent halfway through a run. With StepPersistence, a new process continued from the last settled step. 💾

StepPersistence saves a snapshot of the message history at every settled step, to a store you choose: memory, files, SQLite, MongoDB or Postgres. After a crash, continue_run() loads the latest complete snapshot, and you pass it to agent.run() in a new process. I killed the agent right after its first shell command. The new process continued from there, edited the file and ran the tests.

- It keeps a ledger of tool calls. A call marked started with no result may or may not have run, and you decide if redoing it is safe.
- fork_run() branches a new run from a snapshot.
- There's no server or engine to run.

What it doesn't do is retry the step that was in flight. Tonight, durable execution does.
