Temporal is one way to make the coding agent durable. Pydantic AI supports eight engines. 🔁

Each engine journals the agent's model requests and the tool calls it wraps. A restarted run replays the finished ones and retries the one in flight. Six of them are a capability on the agent, so switching between them is one line, as in the image. Pick the one that matches what you already run.

- Temporal, DBOS and Prefect ship in Pydantic AI.
- AWS Lambda, Absurd and pgtask ship in Pydantic AI Harness. Absurd and pgtask only need Postgres.
- Restate and Airflow ship in their own packages, as RestateAgent and AgentOperator(durable=True).
- An agent has one engine at a time. Adding a second raises an error.
- For any other engine, there's a builder.
