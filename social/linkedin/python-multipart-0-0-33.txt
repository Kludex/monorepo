python-multipart 0.0.33 is out! 🚀

The highlight is streaming limits for URL-encoded forms. FormParser now accepts MAX_FIELDS and MAX_FIELD_SIZE, and raises FormParserError before an oversized field is buffered. Both are opt-in, and multipart forms are unaffected.

Also in this release:

- Uploads without a filename no longer crash when UPLOAD_KEEP_FILENAME or UPLOAD_KEEP_EXTENSIONS is set.
- Quoted-printable escapes split across writes decode correctly, and decoding finishes before your callbacks run.
- parse_options_header() lowercases content types that have parameters.
- Faster boundary matching, parser state lookups, and querystring callbacks.
- Python 3.10 support is dropped. python-multipart now requires Python 3.11.

Thanks to everyone who contributed, and to my GitHub sponsors. 💜
