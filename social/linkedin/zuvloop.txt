I wrote zuvloop, a drop-in replacement for the asyncio event loop, powered by libuv and written in Zig ⚡

Your code stays the same. Call zuvloop.run(main()), or pass loop_factory=zuvloop.new_event_loop to asyncio.run(). Scheduling, timers, sockets and DNS run in native code, on the same libuv engine behind Node.js.

The chart compares it with uvloop, rloop and stock asyncio on CPython 3.14:

- Uvicorn plaintext: 70.1k req/s, vs 47.6k on asyncio and 65.6k on uvloop.
- call_soon_threadsafe: 9.21M/s, over 20x asyncio's 407.7k/s.
- Timer schedule and cancel: 8.79M/s, vs 1.09M/s on asyncio.

It ships OpenTelemetry instrumentation that costs nothing until you configure a provider: slow-callback spans, loop metrics and an unhandled-exception counter. Uvicorn can run on it with --loop zuvloop. It needs Python 3.14+.
