Over the next five days I'm building a coding agent with Pydantic AI, one capability per post. 🛠️

It starts small. The agent in the image has one job: find out why tests/test_parser.py fails and fix the bug. Coder() gives it tools to read, edit and search files and to run shell commands. LocalWorkspace('.') says where: the current directory on my machine. I ran it with Claude Sonnet 5.5. It found that parse_duration never cleared its digit buffer, added one line, and ran the tests to confirm.

- Coder is built from FileSystem, Shell, RepoContext and SubAgents, so you can use it whole or take it apart.
- Commands get your PATH, HOME and locale, and nothing else from your environment.

The catch: its shell runs on my laptop. Tonight, it goes in a box.
