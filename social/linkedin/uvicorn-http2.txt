Uvicorn now speaks HTTP/2 ⚡

Uvicorn 0.53.0 added experimental HTTP/2, built on the zttp parser. Install zttp and run uvicorn main:app --http zttp --http2. Each connection is served as HTTP/1.1 or HTTP/2, depending on what the client speaks. Over HTTPS, HTTP/2 is negotiated with ALPN during the TLS handshake. Over cleartext, clients use prior knowledge.

Uvicorn 0.54.0 builds on it:

- Response trailers, through the ASGI http.response.trailers extension. Send metadata such as a checksum after the body.
- 103 Early Hints, through the ASGI http.response.early_hint extension. Tell the browser which resources to fetch before the final response.

It's experimental and off by default. Upgrade-based h2c and WebSockets over HTTP/2 aren't supported yet.
