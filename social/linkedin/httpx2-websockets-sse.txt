HTTPX2 speaks WebSockets and Server-Sent Events out of the box, no extra client library needed. 🔌

Both live on the client you already use for HTTP requests. The WebSocket handshake is a regular request, so your headers, cookies, authentication, proxies and timeouts apply as usual. client.sse() gives you an iterator of events, and event.json() decodes the payload when it is JSON.

- Works with both httpx2.Client and httpx2.AsyncClient.
- Send and receive text, bytes or JSON, with an optional timeout on receive.
- Keepalive pings are sent for you, and a lost connection raises WebSocketNetworkError.
- WebSockets need the ws extra: pip install "httpx2[ws]"

https://pydantic.dev/docs/httpx2/guides/websockets/
