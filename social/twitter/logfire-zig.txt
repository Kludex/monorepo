Logfire speaks OpenTelemetry, so you can send it traces from Zig ⚡

- OpenTelemetry has an official Zig SDK, in alpha
- Point OTEL_EXPORTER_OTLP_ENDPOINT at Logfire
- Pass your write token as the Authorization header
- Runs on Zig 0.16
