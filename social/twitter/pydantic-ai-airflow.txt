Pydantic AI agents can run as durable Apache Airflow tasks 🛟

- AgentOperator(durable=True)
- Each model request and tool call is recorded
- A retry replays finished steps and continues
- On Airflow 3.3+, no storage to configure

https://pydantic.dev/docs/ai/capabilities/durable_execution/airflow/
