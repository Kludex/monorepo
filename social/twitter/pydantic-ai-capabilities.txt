Capabilities are how you extend a Pydantic AI agent 🧩

- Tools, hooks, instructions and model settings in one unit
- Pass them with Agent(capabilities=[...])
- Built-ins like Thinking, WebSearch and Caching
- Capability() bundles your own, no subclass

https://pydantic.dev/docs/ai/capabilities/overview/
