Pydantic AI agents get durable with DBOS, and all you need is a database 💾

- capabilities=[DBOSDurability()]
- Wrap agent.run() in a @DBOS.workflow
- Model and MCP calls are saved to Postgres or SQLite
- A crash resumes from the last completed step

https://pydantic.dev/docs/ai/capabilities/durable_execution/dbos/
