Starlette and Uvicorn were downloaded 1.4 billion times last month. The sponsorships that pay for maintaining
them add up to $129 a month.

That is not anyone's fault but mine. My [sponsors page](/sponsors/) was a tip jar. I never asked companies for
anything, and I never offered them anything in return.

For 2027, I'm changing that.

## What changed this year

This year I got 520% more security reports than last year: 124 so far, against 20 in 2025.

I close a lot of them without an advisory. [Most reports I get are not valid](/blog/security-vocabulary/). But
every one has to be read, reproduced and answered before I know that.

This work doesn't ship a feature. It is also the part of maintenance that matters most to a company running
these projects in production.

## The 2027 fund

The target is $60,000 for 2027, raised through [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/Kludex).

There are two tiers for companies.

| | Supporter | Partner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price | $500 a month | $1,000 a month |
| Logo on the README and docs of both projects | Yes | Yes, with top placement and the docs sidebar |
| Credit in the release notes | Yes | Yes |
| A sponsor badge for your own site | Yes | Yes |
| A social post when you join | Yes | Yes |
| A mention in the yearly report | Yes | Yes |
| Your company description on the sponsors page | No | Yes |
| A week's notice of security releases | No | Yes |
| Priority triage on your issues | No | Yes |
| One private one-hour session a year | No | Yes |

Your company can pay by card, or GitHub can invoice it, so the payment goes through normal procurement.

## What the security notice is

Partners get a week's notice before a security release. The notice names the project, the severity, the affected
versions and the date of the fix.

It does not describe the vulnerability itself. The point is to let your team plan the upgrade, not to give anyone
a head start on the details. For the same reason, a Partner signs a confidentiality term before the first notice.

If a vulnerability is already being exploited, I ship the fix without waiting.

## What priority triage is

I look at a Partner's issues first. It is not a guarantee of a fix or a merge.
